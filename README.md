# 🏎️ Drift Incremental

A high-octane 3D arcade drifting game built with **Three.js** and **JavaScript**. Master the art of drifting to earn XP and money, unlock new tracks, and build a passive income empire with your ghost drivers.

## 🎮 Core Gameplay

- **Short, Intense Runs**: Race any of 5 uniquely crafted tracks and drift your way to glory
- **Realistic Physics**: Feel the weight of your car with spring-based suspension and dynamic body roll
- **Drift to Win**: The more stylish your slides, the more points you earn
- **Incremental Progression**: Upgrade your car, unlock premium tracks, and hire AI drivers for passive income

## 💰 Features

- **5 Unique Tracks** - From country roads to arctic circuits, each with increasing rewards
- **Car Upgrades** - Boost your XP and money multipliers (handling stays arcade-perfect)
- **Ghost Drivers** - Record your best lap, then hire an AI driver to race it on loop and earn while you're away
- **Physics-Based Handling** - Four independent springs per car create satisfying body roll and realistic drifting feel
- **Persistent Progression** - Your upgrades, unlocks, and best times are saved locally

## 🕹️ Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **WASD** / **Arrows** | Drive |
| **R** | Reset car position |
| **C** | Toggle car visibility (for replays) |
| **Tab** | Open Upgrade Shop |
| **T** | Track Selection |

## 🎨 Low-Poly Aesthetic

Flat-shaded, vibrant colors create a clean, arcade feel that never gets old.

## 🚀 Quick Start

Open `index.html` in a modern browser and start drifting! Your progress saves automatically.
